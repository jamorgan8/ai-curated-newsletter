Study Finds AI Use May Reduce Human Persistence

A new study led by UC Berkeley researchers indicates that relying on an AI tool for as little as 10 minutes can significantly impair an individual's ability to persist with challenging tasks once the AI assistance is removed. The research, presented at the Conference on Language Modeling, raises questions about the cognitive impacts of over-reliance on artificial intelligence.
