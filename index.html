Bipartisan Caucus Introduces Anti-Gerrymandering Legislation

The Congressional Problem Solvers Caucus, a bipartisan group of lawmakers, has introduced a package of four bills aimed at preventing gerrymandering and mid-decade redistricting. The proposed legislation includes the Transparent Redistricting Under Stable Timelines (TRUST) Act, which would limit redistricting to once every 10 years after the census. Another bill, the MAP Act, led by Representatives Mike Lawler (R-NY) and Salud Carbajal (D-CA), seeks to establish federal standards for congressional districts, requiring them to have equal populations, be contiguous, reasonably compact, and respect communities of interest. Additionally, the Nonpartisan Congressional Uniformity and Oversight of Redistricting Trials Act, led by Representatives Ryan Mackenzie (R-PA) and Ed Case (D-HI), would mandate federal courts to hear and expedite congressional redistricting challenges. A final bill, championed by Representatives Brian Fitzpatrick (R-PA) and Debbie Dingell (D-MI), would require states to utilize independent commissions for redistricting.
